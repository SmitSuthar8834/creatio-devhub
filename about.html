In short

I lead Creatio CX delivery at Qnovate and have worked hands-on with Creatio since 2022: Freedom UI, low-code CRM and BPM, integrations, Angular components and C# backend work. Companion for Creatio is my personal side project, built for Creatio developers like me.

Why I built it

Anyone who works with Creatio knows the routine: jumping between clio, Git, GitHub, SQL tools, logs and environments, and trying to keep it all in sync in your head. I wanted one place that makes the development loop easier to see, understand and control.

It doesn't try to replace the tools you already use. It sits on top of clio, Git and the GitHub CLI and connects the dots, and it's shaped by real developer workflows and the feedback people send.
